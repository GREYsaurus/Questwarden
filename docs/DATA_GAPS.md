# Data gaps: what we do not know yet

Facts Questwarden could not rebuild from its own sources (docs/DATA_POLICY.md), and values we hold but have not verified. Nothing here is filled in from another add-on's data. Each entry says how to close it. Update this page whenever data is generated, cross-checked or checked in the game.

Status: **0.1.0 development, Phase 2 in progress.** The Classic Era quest database, talent trees, race/class table, trainer and pet data are generated; the first two routes (Human and Orc/Troll, 1-12) are written.
