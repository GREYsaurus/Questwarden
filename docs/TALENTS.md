# Talents

**Coming in Phase 2:** the 51-point Classic Era talent trees, generated from the 1.15.9 client tables, and leveling and level-60 builds (tank, healer and DPS where relevant) for all nine classes, each validated point by point against the trees.

The talent calculator and talent-window suggestions are described, with TBCGuide's TBC builds, in [tbcguide/TALENTS.md](tbcguide/TALENTS.md). Those builds are not applied to Classic Era.
