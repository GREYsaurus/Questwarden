# Patrolling NPCs

Some quest NPCs don't stand still: they walk a path. When the current step needs one of them, the waypoint arrow follows the NPC's path instead of pointing at a spot where it may not be, and the path is dotted on the world map and the minimap. This is navigation only. Questwarden never moves the character, targets the NPC or completes a step because you reached a point.

Setting: **Follow Patrolling NPCs** (Settings → General → Features, on by default). Code: `Core/Patrol.lua`.

## Which NPCs

- **Accept steps:** the quest's start NPCs. **Turn-in steps:** its end NPCs. **Complete steps:** the NPCs its objectives are about (kills, kill credit, and the NPCs that drop its quest items).
- Only NPCs with a path in the bundled data and at most 2 known spawns: a named NPC on its rounds, not a kind of mob that patrols in many places. Those keep their normal waypoint (or the hunting route, docs/ROAMING.md).
- On a complete step, an NPC whose objective is already done is not followed. This needs the quest log text to name the NPC, so it works on English clients; elsewhere the patrol runs until the step is done.
- Not inside instances.

## How the arrow moves

1. It goes to the nearer end of the path. On a loop (both ends within 1.5% of the map of each other) it goes to the nearest point.
2. Within 15 yards of a point it moves on to the next one. At the far end it turns back along the path; on a loop it goes round again.
3. It stops when the step is done: the quest accepted or turned in, the objective done, or the step skipped. The arrow then shows the next step as usual.

Meeting the NPC on the way doesn't stop the patrol: talk to it or kill it and the step does that. With no player position (a loading screen), it holds the current point. A waypoint you set yourself is not replaced. When the NPC patrols, the hunting route for the same step gives way.

The step window shows "*NPC* patrols: the arrow follows its path" and the point number. With the setting off, the step still says that the NPC patrols and may not be at the waypoint.

## Map and minimap

The followed path is drawn as purple dots on the world map (when the map shows the path's zone and *map pins* are on) and on the minimap (when the *minimap pin* is on; dots past the edge are left out). Dots disappear when the patrol ends.

## Data

`Database/Questie/Patrols.lua`, generated by `tools/build.ps1 import-questie` from the waypoints in QuestieDB's NPC table (the same pinned source as the rest of the quest data). For each bundled NPC with waypoints:

- Points at negative coordinates and repeated points are dropped.
- Paths spanning less than 2% of the map are left out (an NPC stepping around a stall or a room).
- Paths are simplified (Ramer-Douglas-Peucker, 0.3% of the map) and rounded to 0.1.

QuestieDB records where an NPC walks, not where it is now, and its paths have not been checked in the game for Questwarden. If a path is wrong, please report it on the issue tracker.
